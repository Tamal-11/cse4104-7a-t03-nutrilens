# Brag Plan: NutriLens

## What is this app?
NutriLens turns a food photo into a Gemini-assisted nutrition estimate, practical cautions, and goal-aware meal guidance.

## The angle
The camera stops being a camera and becomes a calm, useful meal companion. The edit follows the real product loop: take a meal photo, let NutriLens analyze the visible portion, then leave with clear macro estimates and a recommendation that reflects your food goal.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Your meal has more to say." A leaf-green lens resolves into the NutriLens mark, immediately followed by the real product promise: "Snap a meal, know the deal."

## Key moments (the middle)

- A user drops a meal image into the real AI Lens upload interface.
- The scanner locks in, then resolves into a clear report: detected food, calories, protein, carbs, and fat.
- A goal-aware recommendation and scan-history snapshot prove that the result is useful beyond a single estimate.

## Outro / punchline
NutriLens lands with "Snap a meal, know the deal." and the quiet qualifier "Nutrition estimates, not medical advice."

## User flow worth showing
Upload a JPG/PNG/WebP meal photo -> Gemini-assisted food analysis -> review estimated macros, cautions, recommendation, and saved scan history.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: calm, credible food-tech launch in a leafy mobile interface
- Interpretation: clean cards, generous whitespace, confident UI motion, and short feature-first copy with no hype language.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f2f8ed / #daefcc
- Accent: #599b38
- Text: #1c221a / #1a1b1a
- Display font: Inter 900
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the real NutriLens mobile AI Lens/result interface, including its green macro ring and leaf-camera logo.

## Share copy (draft)
NutriLens turns a meal photo into a clear nutrition estimate, useful cautions, and goal-aware guidance. Snap a meal, know the deal.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, upbeat product bed with restrained interface accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (120.19 BPM)
- Music treatment: quiet opening, confident lift under the scan result, then fade beneath the final lockup.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset; beat grid from 3.02s onward. Major locked reveal at 16.02s; result cards arrive at 12.02s, 12.52s, and 13.01s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; leaf-green glow and scanner aura breathe with music energy. No waveform, equalizer, or text pulsing.
- SFX posture: sparse, low-risk UI click for upload and warm impact for the result resolve.
- Audio-coupled moments: upload tap at 4.02s; scan lock at 7.02s; three macro rows at 12.02s/12.52s/13.01s; final logo at 16.02s.
- Restraint rule: do not let SFX or music imply medical certainty or overwhelm readable results.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Meal signal — 3.0s
The NutriLens leaf-camera mark forms over a light green field. Large readable copy: "Your meal has more to say." The smaller product line settles: "Snap a meal, know the deal."
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: a warm bed begins, with a single soft brand arrival.
Audio-coupled idea: soft logo resolve.
Music: confident but low in the mix.
Transition mood: clean leaf-green wipe -> Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Drop the photo — 4.0s
Show the actual NutriLens AI Lens upload screen inside a mobile frame. A cursor drops a meal image onto the dashed upload zone; copy reads "One photo." then "One clear next step."
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor moves, presses, then a meal tile settles into the upload zone.
Audio intent: precise click, then a light anticipation rise.
Audio-coupled idea: simulated upload tap.
Music: continues under a single interface click.
Transition mood: scanner lock -> Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — AI Lens resolves — 4.5s
The phone becomes a focused scan panel. A scan line moves over the meal image; a large label resolves: "Food analysis" and "Gemini-assisted estimate." The ring glows gently with the bed.
Sequential/interaction: yes — scan line crosses, then the status chips switch from analyzing to ready.
Audio intent: soft confirmation, never a medical alarm.
Audio-coupled idea: scan lock lands at 7.02s.
Music: small lift toward the result.
Transition mood: clean panel expansion -> Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The useful part — 4.5s
Show the actual result image alongside recreated, readable cards: "Detected: Chicken rice", "520 kcal", and the Protein/Carbs/Fat estimate. The three macro cards arrive one by one, then hold as a practical caution and recommendation appear.
Sequential/interaction: yes — Protein, Carbs, then Fat cards land on the beat grid at 12.02s, 12.52s, 13.01s and hold together.
Audio intent: three quiet, consistent card accents followed by a warm resolve.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card reveal.
Music: clearest beat section, still secondary to the copy.
Transition mood: smooth slide -> Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Keep the signal — 4.0s
A simplified history/calendar panel appears beside the NutriLens mark: "Scan. Understand. Return." The final lockup reads "NutriLens" and "Snap a meal, know the deal." with the estimate disclaimer below.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three history rows settle in, then the logo locks at 16.02s.
Audio intent: warm final impact, music fades under the held logo.
Audio-coupled idea: logo payoff at 16.02s.
Music: resolve and fade.
Transition mood: final hold.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm, composed.
