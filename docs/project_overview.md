# Project Overview

NutriLens is an AI-based food recognition and nutrition analysis system. It allows users to upload an image of a food item and receive estimated nutrition information including calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, minerals, benefits, and possible side effects.

## Main Goals

- Make food nutrition information easier to understand.
- Help users identify food from images.
- Provide quick estimated nutrition values.
- Warn users about possible risks of excessive consumption.
- Support healthier food choices.

## Main Users

- Students
- General users
- Fitness-conscious people
- People tracking food habits
- Developers testing AI food recognition systems
